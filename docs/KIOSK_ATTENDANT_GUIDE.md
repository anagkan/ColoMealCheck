## Quick guide for a busy meal

### A normal member check-in

1. Ask the member to hold their TigerCard flat against the reader.
2. Wait for the result. Do not ask them to tap repeatedly.
3. A **green** message means the meal was recorded.
4. An **amber** message means the meal was recorded, but the system noticed an overage or warning. Let the member continue and tell a manager if needed.
5. Read a **red** message carefully. Some red messages are warnings about the membership but still say the meal was recorded. Other red messages say that nothing was recorded.

If the card will not scan, enter the member's **9-digit Princeton ID (PUID)** in the box on the main screen and press **Check in**.

### If a card is not recognized

1. Confirm that the member used the correct card.
2. Select **Staff: link this card**.
3. Follow the enrollment instructions beginning on page 3 of this guide.

### A guest meal

The easiest method is:

1. Have the hosting member check in or tap their card.
2. Select **+ Guest**.
3. Enter the guest's first name, last name, and Princeton NetID.
4. If the guest has no NetID, select **Guest has no NetID** and enter the reason.
5. Select **Record guest meal**.

### An alumni meal

1. Select **Alumni Meal** at the bottom of the main screen.
2. Enter the alum's name and four-digit class year.
3. Enter an email address or phone number. At least one is required.
4. Enter the alum's NetID if they still have one. This is optional.
5. Select **Record alumni meal**.

### The three messages that require special attention

- **Offline — _number_ queued:** The kiosk saved the meals and will send them later. Do not enter them again.
- **_Number_ scans not recorded:** Select the red message, tell a manager, and do not clear the list until every item has been entered through **Admin → Attendance**.
- **No meal is being served right now:** This is about the schedule, not the internet. If the person is actually eating, choose the correct nearby meal shown on the screen.

### At the end of your shift

- Return to the main **Tap your TigerCard** screen.
- Look for an orange offline queue or a red “not recorded” message in the header.
- Tell the next attendant or a manager about any unresolved message.
- Leave the kiosk plugged in when possible.

# 1. What the kiosk does

The kiosk records who eats each meal at Colonial Club. A member can check in with either:

- their TigerCard; or
- the 9-digit PUID printed on their TigerCard.

The system connects the check-in to the member's name, meal plan, membership status, and the meal currently being served. It also keeps separate records for guest meals and alumni meals.

The main screen shows the current meal near the top. If no meal is open, it shows the next meal or says **Outside Meal Hours**. A result stays on the screen briefly and then returns to the main screen automatically.

## What the colors mean

### Green: recorded normally

The meal was recorded. The screen may also show how many meals the member has used that week and how many guest meals remain.

### Amber: recorded with a warning

The meal was still recorded. The most common reason is that the member is over their weekly meal-plan allowance. **Do not make the member tap again.** Meal-plan overages are handled later by management.

### Red: stop and read the words

Red does not always mean the same thing. Read the complete message.

- **Card not recognized:** No meal was recorded. The card needs to be linked to a member.
- **No meal is being served right now:** No meal was recorded yet. Choose the correct nearby meal if the person is actually eating.
- **Membership warning:** The meal may already have been recorded. If the message says it was recorded, do not scan again. Tell a manager about the warning.
- **Already checked in:** A second meal was not recorded. The guest form may open automatically because a second tap often means the member is bringing a guest.

## Weekly and monthly counts

- A member's meal-plan count starts over each **Monday**.
- Unused meals do not roll into the next week.
- Guest allowances are separate from the member's own meal plan.
- Guest allowances start over on the **first day of each month**.
- The number shown by the kiosk is the current rule. Do not rely on a remembered number if the screen says something different.

# 2. Checking in a member

## With a TigerCard

1. Make sure the main screen says **Tap your TigerCard**.
2. Ask the member to place the card flat against the reader for a moment.
3. Wait for the name and result to appear.
4. Read the result before taking another action.

One tap is enough. Repeated taps can look like a duplicate check-in and may open the guest form.

## With a PUID

Use this when the card will not read but the member knows their Princeton ID.

1. Select the box labeled **Enter your Princeton ID**.
2. Enter all 9 digits. Use the PUID printed on the TigerCard, not the NetID.
3. Select **Check in** or press Enter.
4. Read the result.

Entering a PUID records the same kind of check-in as tapping a card. A member whose card has not yet been linked can still check in by PUID if they are already on the roster.

## Correcting an immediate mistake

If the wrong person or meal was recorded and the **Undo** button is still visible, select **Undo** immediately. The screen should say **Check-in undone**.

If the result has already disappeared, do not create another transaction to “balance it out.” Write down the person's name, the approximate time, and what happened. Ask a manager to correct it in the Admin area.

## Checking in just before or after a meal

When no meal is currently open, the kiosk may offer the meal that just ended and the meal that will start next.

1. Confirm that the person is actually eating now.
2. Read both choices carefully.
3. Select the meal that the person is eating.

This records the meal against the correct service date. Do not choose a nearby meal merely to remove the warning.

# 3. Enrolling a new member and TigerCard

Enrollment creates a member record and links a TigerCard to it. Use the **New member** choice only when the person is not already in the system.

## Information to collect

Ask the member for:

- first and last name;
- **PUID:** exactly 9 numbers;
- **NetID:** 2–8 letters and numbers, beginning with a letter;
- class year, if known;
- meal plan; and
- their TigerCard.

Do not guess a meal plan. Ask the member or confirm it with a manager. The choice already shown in the box is only a default.

## Starting enrollment

There are two ways to open the enrollment page:

- If an unrecognized card was just tapped, select **Staff: link this card**. The card number will already be filled in.
- From the main screen, select **Enroll** in the upper-right corner. Then have the member tap their card in the card section.

The card field should contain 16 characters made from the numbers 0–9 and letters A–F. The card reader normally fills this automatically. Do not try to copy the long number unless the reader cannot be used.

## Enrolling the member

1. Enter the **staff PIN** in Step 1. Do not say the PIN aloud or let the member watch you enter it.
2. In Step 2, confirm that the card field is filled. If necessary, ask the member to tap the card once.
3. In Step 3, keep **New member** selected.
4. Enter the member's first and last name.
5. Enter the 9-digit PUID.
6. Enter the NetID. It is required for a new enrollment.
7. Enter the four-digit class year if known.
8. Choose the correct meal plan:
   - **19 meals**
   - **14 meals**
   - **RCA/PAA (9 meals)**
   - **No plan**
9. In Step 4, add a photo if practical. A photo is helpful at the door but is optional.
10. Review the information with the member.
11. Select **Enroll member** once.
12. Wait for the green success message. It will say that the form has been cleared for the next person.

The staff PIN stays filled for the next enrollment, but the member and card fields are cleared. Protect the screen while the PIN remains active.

## Taking a photo

1. Select **Start camera**.
2. Ask the member to face the camera. Frame the head and shoulders clearly.
3. Select **Take photo**.
4. Check the preview. Select **Retake** if necessary.

If the camera is unavailable, select **Upload a file**, or continue without a photo. Do not delay a meal solely because a photo cannot be taken.

## If the page refuses the enrollment

Read the message beside the field or at the bottom of the page. Correct that item and try once more.

Common causes are:

- the PUID is not exactly 9 digits;
- the NetID is not in the correct format;
- the card did not produce all 16 characters;
- the PUID or NetID already belongs to another member;
- the card is already linked to somebody; or
- the staff PIN is incorrect.

The system refuses conflicts instead of guessing or joining two people together. **Do not change information until the error disappears by trial and error.** Confirm the person's identity, then ask a manager if the conflict remains.

When finished, use **Cancel** or **Back to check-in** to return to the main kiosk screen.

# 4. Linking a replacement TigerCard

Use this process when a person is already in the system but has received a new or replacement TigerCard. Do not create a second member record.

1. Select **Enroll** from the main screen.
2. Enter the staff PIN.
3. Have the member tap the **new** card in the card section.
4. In Step 3, select **Already in the system**.
5. Search for the member by name or PUID.
6. Carefully choose the correct person from the results. Check the PUID, NetID, and class year shown beside the name.
7. Confirm that the page says **Linking to** the correct member.
8. A new photo may be added, but it is optional.
9. Select **Link card**.
10. Wait for the success message.

The old card is retired automatically. It will no longer check that member in. If you selected the wrong person, stop and tell a manager rather than trying to repair the records yourself.

# 5. Recording a guest meal

A guest meal belongs to a hosting member but does not use one of the member's own weekly meals.

## Recommended method

1. Have the host tap their TigerCard or complete their own check-in.
2. On the result screen, select **+ Guest**.
3. Confirm that the correct host is shown.
4. Enter the guest's first and last name.
5. Enter the guest's Princeton NetID.
6. Select **Record guest meal**.

If a member taps a second time during the same meal, the kiosk may open this guest form automatically. If they do not have a guest, select **Cancel**.

## Using the Guest Meal button

The **Guest Meal** button at the bottom of the main screen opens the same form without a host filled in.

1. Search for the hosting member by name or PUID.
2. Select the correct member from the search results.
3. Enter the guest's information and record the meal.

## A guest without a NetID

Some guests, such as parents or siblings, do not have a Princeton NetID.

1. Select **Guest has no NetID**.
2. In the reason box, enter a short clear reason, such as “visiting parent.”
3. Complete the remaining fields and record the guest meal.

Never leave both the NetID and reason blank.

## When the guest allowance has been used

The kiosk will show that the guest quota has been reached and will ask for a staff PIN and a reason before it allows an override.

Only override the quota if club policy allows it or a manager approves it. Enter a specific reason, not only “approved.” The override is saved in the audit record.

# 6. Recording an alumni meal

An alumni meal is not charged to a current member and does not use a guest allowance.

1. Select **Alumni Meal** at the bottom of the main screen.
2. Enter the alum's first and last name.
3. Enter the four-digit class year.
4. Enter at least one way to contact the alum:
   - email address; or
   - phone number.
5. Enter the Princeton NetID if the alum still has one. This field is optional and does not replace the contact information.
6. Select **Record alumni meal**.
7. Wait for the recorded message before closing the form.

# 7. Working during a network problem

The kiosk can temporarily save check-ins even when it cannot reach the server.

## Orange “Offline — _number_ queued” message

This means the kiosk saved one or more meals on the Chromebook and is waiting to send them to the server.

- The screen may say **Saved offline** without showing the person's name or photo. That is normal because the server is what looks up the card.
- Tell the person the meal was saved.
- **Do not scan the card again.** A second scan can create another queued request.
- Leave the kiosk running and connected. It retries automatically.
- The queue survives a browser restart or Chromebook restart.

Member search does not work offline. To record a guest while offline, have the host tap their card first, then select **+ Guest**. The kiosk saves the host's card with the guest meal and resolves the name later.

## Red “scan not recorded” message

Sometimes a queued item reaches the server later but cannot be accepted. Examples include an unrecognized card, a guest allowance problem, or an item that remained offline too long.

1. Select the red **scan not recorded** message in the header.
2. Read the name or card number, time, and reason.
3. Tell a manager.
4. Add the missing meal through **Admin → Attendance**, or have an authorized manager do it.
5. Only after the meal has been entered, select **Clear list — these have been entered**.

Clearing this list does not create an attendance record. It only removes the warning, so never clear it first.

## If the entire page will not load

1. Wait about one minute in case the connection returns.
2. Check that the Chromebook is connected to power and Wi-Fi.
3. Do not change the kiosk's web address.
4. Contact the House Manager or technical contact.
5. If meals cannot be recorded electronically, use the emergency paper log. Record the time, meal, person's full name, PUID if available, and whether the meal was for a member, guest, or alum.

Protect the paper log as personal information and give it directly to a manager for entry when the system returns.

# 8. Troubleshooting and good habits

## The card does nothing

1. Ask the member to hold it flat against the reader for a moment.
2. Try once more, not many times.
3. If there is still no response, use the 9-digit PUID box.
4. If **Reader bridge offline** appears, tell the technical contact and use PUID entry until it is fixed.

If the screen responds with **Card not recognized**, the reader worked. The card simply is not linked yet; follow the enrollment instructions.

## The wrong name appears

Do not let the person continue under somebody else's name. Use **Undo** if it is still available, write down what happened, and tell a manager. The card may have been linked to the wrong person.

## The guest or alumni form will not save

Read the red message in the form. It normally identifies a missing or invalid field. Do not keep selecting the record button without correcting the named problem.

## The screen is black or asleep

1. Make sure the charger is connected.
2. Open the lid if it is closed.
3. Press a key once and wait several seconds.
4. If it does not return, contact the technical contact.

## Battery indicator

The small percentage in the header shows the Chromebook battery. A lightning symbol means it is charging. Connect the charger when the indicator becomes amber or red.

## Protect member information

- Do not share or write the staff PIN on this guide.
- Do not leave the Enrollment or Admin page open after using it.
- Do not photograph the screen or copy member information for personal use.
- Keep paper logs private and give them to a manager promptly.
- Use the system only for real meals. Test scans create real attendance records unless they are undone.

## Before leaving the kiosk

1. Finish or cancel any open guest, alumni, or enrollment form.
2. Return to the **Tap your TigerCard** screen.
3. Check for orange queued items or red unrecorded items.
4. Report anything unresolved to the next attendant or manager.
5. Leave the Chromebook plugged in when possible. When unplugged, closing the lid puts it to sleep; when plugged in, it is configured to remain available.
